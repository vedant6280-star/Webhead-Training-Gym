🛡️ Training Protocol

  • Warm up with 5 minutes of easy walking or cycling.
  • Ask a gym trainer to adjust machines and demonstrate safe technique.
  • Start with 1–2 sets of 10 comfortable repetitions per machine.
  • Rest 60–120 seconds between sets, or longer if needed.
  • Use a weight you can control without holding your breath.
  • Stop if you experience pain, dizziness or unusual breathlessness.
  • Eat regular, balanced meals, drink water and get enough sleep.
Important: You are a beginner, so there is no need to lift heavy or push through pain. Consistency matters more than intensity. Avoid crash diets.
